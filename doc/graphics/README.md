# SenSketch Google Play graphics

`feature_graphic.svg` is the editable source for the Google Play feature graphic. It is designed at **1024 × 500 px** and keeps the app name and main illustration away from the outer edges.

The design uses SenSketch's dark canvas and light continuous stroke as a visual preview of the app. Text and all artwork remain editable in Inkscape or another SVG editor. The English tagline is placeholder copy and can be changed in the SVG.

## Google Play upload

Google Play currently accepts a **1024 × 500 px JPEG or 24-bit PNG without transparency** for the feature graphic. SVG is useful as the editable source, but it cannot be uploaded directly. When the design is approved, export the page from the SVG at exactly 1024 × 500 and use an opaque background when exporting PNG.

This is a draft for review. It has not been applied to the Android app icon or included in an app build.
